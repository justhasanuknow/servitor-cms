# Code of Conduct

Servitor CMS is made by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Everybody who takes part in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces should feel welcome and safe.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- being kind, patient and respectful, also when you disagree;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback about the work, not the person;
- assuming good intent and asking before drawing conclusions;
- making room for newcomers and for people who write in their second language;
- owning mistakes and learning from them.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks;
- discriminatory jokes or language, and unwelcome sexual attention or content;
- publishing other people's private information without their permission;
- trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or continuing to contact someone who asked you to stop;
- any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers, through the contact details on the GitHub profile of the repository owner, or with GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially, and nobody is penalized for reporting in good faith.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban people who do so, depending on the severity and on whether the behavior continues.
